Howl round 01: directions

Three ways Howl's home screen could look. Each uses #CC0000 for buttons and the Howl button. Reply and Pack numbers are sample data.

A · Bellow

White and quiet, with every post set in heavy type so it reads like a shout. Borrows Threads' text-first feed and Bluesky's tabs and round write button.

B · Roll Call

True black and dense, with the place name leading every post as a headline. Borrows X's tight rows and Instagram's row of circles, here used to pick a place.

C · After Hours

Deep navy with warm cream cards, where short posts are set big and long ones shrink to reading size. Borrows Tumblr's cards and Follow button and Instagram's small bold names.
